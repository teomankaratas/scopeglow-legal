SCOPEGLOW

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Short version: Your proposals, client details, brand profiles, images, PDFs, and backups stay on your device. ScopeGlow sends only subscription and purchase information needed to provide ScopeGlow Pro to Google Play and RevenueCat.

Information stored on your device

ScopeGlow stores the information you enter—such as client and company names, project details, services, prices, contact information, brand profiles, logos, portfolio images, proposal status, and reminders—in private app storage on your device. ScopeGlow has no account system and no developer-operated cloud backend. We do not receive this proposal content.

Subscriptions

If you view, buy, or restore ScopeGlow Pro, Google Play processes the transaction and RevenueCat, Inc. helps validate the purchase and keep your Pro entitlement current. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an automatically generated anonymous app user identifier, together with limited transaction context such as locale and currency. ScopeGlow does not send RevenueCat your proposal content, client details, portfolio images, or contact details.

How information is used

  • On-device information is used only to provide the features you request, including previews, PDFs, reminders, local analytics, and backups.
  • Purchase information is used for app functionality, subscription restoration, fraud prevention, and aggregate subscription analytics.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

Your choices and deletion

You choose when to export or share a PDF or backup. You can delete individual proposals inside the app. Uninstalling ScopeGlow removes its local app data subject to Android backup behavior. To request deletion of a RevenueCat customer record or ask a privacy question, open a private request using the contact link below and include the anonymous app user identifier shown by support instructions.

Security and retention

Local content remains in Android app storage until you delete it or remove the app. Subscription data is encrypted in transit and retained by Google Play and RevenueCat according to their service, legal, fraud-prevention, and financial record obligations.

Children

ScopeGlow is a business productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy when ScopeGlow's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
